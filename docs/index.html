About this bot

The opponent is a Double DQN agent: a small convolutional network trained with reinforcement learning to find mines on random boards. It runs entirely in your browser through ONNX Runtime Web, with no backend.

It sees only what you see: covered cells, clue numbers and mines already found. For each covered cell it estimates how good a pick would be, and plays the best one.

Difficulty

Difficulty is the temperature of a softmax over those estimates. Hard always plays the top cell. Medium and Easy sample from the distribution.

Each level's behaviour and the turns it needs alone on a fresh board
LevelBehaviourTurns alone
EasySlips often (high temperature)about 17
MediumSlips sometimes (low temperature)about 12.5
HardAlways plays its best guess (greedy)about 10

Random clicking needs about 23 turns, an exact probability solver need about 9.9 turns.
